The tradition

What chicory coffee is.

Chicory coffee is coffee blended with the roasted, ground root of the chicory plant.

New Orleans took to it when coffee was scarce and chicory stretched the supply. The shortage ended. The taste stayed.

Chicory adds body and a gentle caramel sweetness, so the cup drinks fuller than the coffee alone. Chicory root has no caffeine, so a coffee-and-chicory cup carries a little less than straight coffee.

St. Roch Blend

How we do it.

St. Roch Blend is our coffee and chicory. It uses a higher ratio of coffee to chicory than most blends, so you get the tradition without losing the coffee. Cocoa body, caramelized chicory sweetness, soft balanced finish. The coffee is roasted on century-old Royal No. 6 drum roasters on the Mississippi Gulf Coast.

Born at St. Roch Market. Now poured at every CR Coffee Shop.

Common questions.

Where can I buy chicory coffee in New Orleans?

At CR Coffee Shop. We pour St. Roch Blend, our own coffee and chicory, at Magazine Street, St. Roch Market, Old Metairie, and our kiosk at the MSY airport. Bags are on the shelf at the neighborhood shops and in the online store.

Does CR Coffee sell chicory coffee by the bag?

Yes. St. Roch Blend comes in a 12 oz bag, whole bean or ground, for $19.75. It is roasted the week it ships and ships anywhere in the U.S.

What does chicory coffee taste like?

Fuller and a little sweeter than the same coffee without chicory. St. Roch Blend tastes of cocoa first, then caramelized chicory sweetness, with a soft balanced finish and no bitterness.

Is a café au lait made with chicory coffee?

The New Orleans version is. Ours is equal parts hot drip St. Roch Blend and steamed milk, and it is on the menu at every shop.
