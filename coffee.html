The machines

A pair of Royal No. 6 drum roasters, built in the 1910s.

Still working every week, they are some of the only century-old roasters running in America. The drums spin slowly over open flame, letting sugars caramelize before the beans get oily. That patience is why the cup comes out naturally sweeter and smoother, with none of the bitterness modern speed roasting leaves behind.

CR pours Coast Roast Coffee exclusively. Kevin co-founded the roastery in 2009 with Shawn Montella, who owns and runs it on the Mississippi Gulf Coast, and Kevin picks every coffee we serve and sell.

Common questions.

Does CR Coffee roast its own beans?

CR serves Coast Roast Coffee exclusively. Kevin co-founded Coast Roast in 2009 with Shawn Montella, who owns and runs the roastery on the Mississippi Gulf Coast. Kevin selects every coffee CR pours and sells, and every batch is roasted on antique Royal No. 6 drum roasters.

What makes CR Coffee different from other New Orleans coffee shops?

The roasting. Antique 1910s drum roasters spin slowly instead of blasting through batches in minutes, so sugars caramelize naturally and the cup comes out sweeter and smoother without bitterness. The beans come through the Port of New Orleans and direct farm relationships in Central and South America.

Where can I buy CR Coffee beans online?

Every blend ships nationwide from crcoffeenola.com, whole bean or ground, roasted close to ship date. Gift cards and merch are in the shop year-round.
