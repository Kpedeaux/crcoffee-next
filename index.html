Meet the Royal No. 6.

Our two Royal No. 6 roasters were built in the 1910s, cast iron and open flame. Green coffee comes up through the Port of New Orleans, and every batch is roasted slow, by hand and by ear, the way it was done a century ago. It reaches our shops the same week it leaves the drum.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

What time does CR Coffee open?

Magazine Street is open daily from 6am to 7pm. St. Roch Market pours daily from 7am to 7pm. Metairie Road opens weekdays 6am to 6pm and weekends 7am to 6pm. Hours at the MSY Airport kiosk vary by terminal.

Can I order ahead online?

Yes. Order through our Square store and your drink is ready at the counter when you walk in at Magazine Street or Old Metairie. St. Roch Market takes orders through its own store, linked on that location's page.

Does CR Coffee have Wi-Fi and seating for laptops?

Yes. Magazine Street has fast Wi-Fi, indoor seating with a fireplace lounge, and a shaded side patio, about ten minutes from Tulane and Loyola.

Is CR Coffee dog friendly?

Yes. Magazine Street has a dog-friendly courtyard with outdoor seating and a covered porch.

Do you have oat milk or almond milk?

Yes. Any drink made with milk, from lattes to chai, can be made with whole, skim, oat, or almond milk.

Where can I buy CR Coffee beans?

All five blends are $19.75 for a 12 ounce bag, whole bean or ground. Pick up a bag at the counter, or order online and we ship anywhere in the U.S., roasted close to the ship date.

Does CR Coffee sell gift cards?

Yes. Digital CR Coffee gift cards are available online and deliver by email, for birthdays, holidays, or just because.

Does CR Coffee have a rewards program?

Yes. CR Coffee Rewards earns 1 Bean for every dollar at Magazine Street and Old Metairie, and 100 Beans earns a free drink. No app and no card. Give your name or phone number at the register and you are enrolled.
