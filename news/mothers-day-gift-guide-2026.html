A CR Coffee Mother's Day Gift Guide

Mother's Day is Sunday, May 10 this year. If your mom is the coffee drinker in your family, and in New Orleans, odds are she is, you've got a better shot at getting her something she'll actually use than with another bouquet that's wilted by Wednesday.

By Kevin Pedeaux

My grandmother used to put coffee in my milk when I was a kid. That's not a joke, that's just how we did it. So this year, instead of sending you to a department store, I pulled together a gift guide of what we actually sell at CR that I'd grab off my own shelf for the moms in my life.

A few of these you can order online and have shipped. A few you need to come in for.

The Easy Win: Streetcar Blend

If your mom drinks a pot of coffee every morning and you don't want to overthink this, get her Streetcar. It's our medium roast. It's what we pour at every CR location every single day. Full body, light acidity, smooth and satisfying finish. It's the coffee that converts people who say they don't like medium roasts.

The honest case for Streetcar as a gift: it's reliable. You're not asking her to learn a new flavor profile or change her routine. You're just upgrading the bag on her counter to something roasted that same week on a pair of 1910s drum roasters on the Gulf Coast. She'll notice. She might not say it the first morning, but by day four when the bag is half gone she'll be wondering why her regular coffee doesn't taste like this.

Twelve-ounce bag, whole bean or ground. Order it online or grab one at any location.

The Nostalgic Pick: St. Roch Blend

If your mom is from here, or if she spent enough time here to develop opinions about café au lait, get her St. Roch Blend. This is our coffee and chicory blend. It's the New Orleans cup, but the way I wanted it to taste, not the way it usually does.

Here's the thing about most coffee-and-chicory in town: the chicory drowns the coffee. It's heavy, it's bitter, and half the time you're tasting the chicory first and the coffee second. We went the other way. Lighter roast on the coffee side, less chicory in the ratio, so what you get is a round, cocoa-forward cup with chicory as a gentle caramel note on the finish. Add hot milk and you've got a café au lait that tastes like your grandmother's kitchen but better.

This is the bag that hits hardest for moms who grew up drinking coffee the New Orleans way. It's a memory in a bag, and that's hard to beat as a gift.

The "She Deserves Something Special" Pick: Venezuelan Single Origin

For the coffee nerd mom, or the mom who's gotten into specialty coffee in the last few years, grab her a bag of our Venezuelan single origin. I wrote about this one a couple weeks ago, it's the first Venezuelan coffee I've had on the shelf in twenty years. 100% Arabica from the Sanare region, sweet caramel and cocoa with just a whisper of fruit on the finish.

What makes this one a good Mother's Day gift specifically is the story. You're not just handing her a bag of beans. You're handing her a bag of coffee that basically didn't exist in the American market for two decades and just reappeared. There's a conversation in this gift. And when it's gone, I genuinely don't know when we'll see the next lot.

Whole bean, twelve-ounce bag. If you're going to order one thing off this list that won't be here next Mother's Day, make it this. Order Venezuelan online.

The Mom Who Drinks Iced Coffee All Year: Cold Brew Blend

Some moms drink iced coffee twelve months a year. If that's yours, our Cold Brew Blend is the move. One twelve-ounce bag makes a full quart of concentrate, which means she's got cold brew ready to pour over ice all week with no effort past the initial steep.

Grind it coarse, steep eighteen hours on the counter, cut it one-to-one with water or milk in the morning. It's the same blend we use in the shop for our cold brew, and it's built to stay smooth and chocolatey even when the New Orleans heat is doing its thing. Pair it with a French press or any of the cold brew gear we recommend and you've turned her kitchen into a low-effort coffee shop.

The Upgrade Gift: A Real Coffee Grinder

This is the one I want to talk you into even if you didn't think of it. If your mom has been drinking pre-ground coffee her whole life, the single biggest upgrade you can give her isn't better beans, it's a better grinder. Fresh-ground coffee from a cheap bag still beats old-ground coffee from a great bag.

I've been recommending the Baratza Virtuoso+ to customers for years, and I hear back about it constantly. People tell me years later they still use it every morning. Metal body, quiet, heavy enough to stay put, and simple enough that you set the grind size once and never touch it again. One button in the morning.

Pair it with a bag of Streetcar and you've given her a complete morning coffee upgrade. She'll taste the difference the first day. The full gear page has my other picks if you want to go further, the Moccamaster for a full pot, the Clever dripper for single cups, the Chemex if she appreciates a beautiful object on the counter.

The In-Person Gift: Take Her to the Porch

Not everything has to ship in a box. If your mom is local, the best Mother's Day gift I can suggest is the one that doesn't cost you shipping: take her to the porch on Magazine Street, buy her a Sweet Vanilla Cream Cold Brew, and sit with her for an hour. No phones if you can help it. The patio is open for the season and it's the best seat in Uptown on a spring Sunday.

If she's an uptown mom, Magazine. If she's an old Metairie mom, Metairie Road. If she loves a market, St. Roch. Pick her spot and show up.

The Gift Card Bailout

Look, some of you are reading this the morning of. I've been there. We sell CR Coffee gift cards at every location and online, and she can spend it on coffee, drinks, a bag of beans, whatever she wants. No shipping delays, no guesswork. Sign her up for CR Rewards while you're at it and every dollar she spends from here on out earns her something back.

One More Thing

Mother's Day falls on Sunday this year, which means if you're ordering online for shipping, don't wait until Friday. Get your order in by the middle of the week so it's on her counter when she wakes up. If you're coming in person, we'll be open all weekend at every location.

And if you need help picking, just come see me or any of our baristas. We do this all day. We know what we like, we know what our moms like, and we'll point you at the right bag.

Happy Mother's Day from all of us at CR. Go call your mom.
