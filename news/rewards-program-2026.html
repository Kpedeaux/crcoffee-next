Your Coffee Has Been Paying Off: How CR Coffee Rewards Works

Your coffee runs have been paying off, and a lot of folks don't realize how fast the Beans actually stack up. So here is the whole thing in plain English.

By Kevin Pedeaux

Every dollar you spend at CR Coffee earns you one Bean. That goes for any of our locations and for orders placed on CRCoffeeNOLA.com. Beans stack up automatically in your Square Loyalty account. No card in your wallet, no app on your phone. Just tell us your name or punch in your phone number at the register and you're in.

Hit 100 Beans and your next drink is on us, anything on the menu up to seven dollars. Hit 200 Beans and we'll send you home with a free bag of our fresh-roasted coffee. Streetcar, French Roast, Cold Brew Blend, your pick.

Shot Tiers: your status, upgraded

Here is the part that makes the regulars happy. Your Bean count doesn't just earn you rewards, it unlocks your status.

Everybody starts on Single Shot: one Bean per dollar, simple math. Once you cross 200 Beans, you're bumped up to Double Shot and you start earning 1.2 Beans per dollar, which is twenty percent faster. Keep going and at 500 Beans you hit Triple Shot, which earns at 1.33x. That's a free drink roughly every 75 dollars instead of every 100.

Your tier stays active as long as you keep spending within the calendar year. If you're in here a few times a week, you'll land on Triple Shot and stay there.

Signing up takes about ten seconds

Next time you're at Magazine Street or Metairie Road, the cashier will prompt you at checkout. Type in your phone number, enter your name, done. You're enrolled and already earning Beans on that purchase. If you'd rather do it at home, the link below takes you straight to the sign-up page.

That's it. Buy the coffee you were already going to buy, and every so often one is free. We don't run gimmicks, we don't send you seventeen emails a week. Just Beans, Shots, and free coffee.
