The story

Iced, on purpose.

It's still a thousand degrees out, so no, we're not handing you a hot pumpkin spice latte in September. The Bayou Beast is the answer instead: a pandan iced latte topped with warm fall spices, cold as the swamp with just enough autumn to remind you the heat won't last forever.

He only prowls from mid-July through Halloween, and then he's gone until next year. Consider yourself warned.

What's pandan?

The leaf

Southeast Asia

New to pandan? It's a Southeast Asian leaf, sort of the vanilla of that part of the world: sweet, grassy, a little coconut. It's what makes the Beast taste like nothing else on the menu, and it's where that swamp-green color comes from.

The build

Iced latte

Espresso pulled from our CR Espresso Blend, cold milk, pandan, and a finish of warm fall spices on top. Green on the bottom, coffee on top, until you stir.
