The market stall

Inside the market.

This is where it all started. CR Coffee opened inside St. Roch Market at 2381 St. Claude Avenue back in 2015, and it's still the neighborhood spot where locals grab Coast Roast Coffee and catch up. Tucked between the Marigny and the Upper Ninth Ward, the market sits in the heart of the St. Roch neighborhood.

The counter is always busy with roasted beans on display, ready to take home or brew fresh. The market itself has food stalls, wine, and the kind of energy that makes St. Claude feel alive. Come for the coffee, stay for the whole scene.
