Streetcar Blend

Bold. Balanced. Built for New Orleans.

The blend

The medium roast we serve every day.

Streetcar is the medium roast we serve every day at all CR Coffee Shop locations. It's rich, full-bodied, and reliably smooth. This isn't your typical light-bodied medium roast. Streetcar hits with satisfying richness in the cup, a smooth full body, and just a touch of light acidity to keep things lively. The kind of coffee that pairs beautifully with breakfast, a quiet moment, or your favorite corner table.

Tasting notes.

Full body, light acidity, smooth and satisfying finish.

Roast: Medium

Origin: Central and South American blend
