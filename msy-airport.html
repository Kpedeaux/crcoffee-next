Common questions.

Where can I get coffee at the New Orleans airport?

CR Coffee is inside Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport (MSY) at 1 Terminal Dr, Kenner, LA 70062. Serving the same Coast Roast Coffee roasted on antique 1910s Royal No. 6 drum roasters.

What are the hours for CR Coffee at MSY Airport?

Hours vary by terminal schedule. The location serves travelers arriving and departing New Orleans.

Is there locally owned coffee at the New Orleans airport?

Yes. CR Coffee Shop at MSY is locally owned and operated, serving the same antique-roasted Coast Roast Coffee available at all four CR Coffee locations across New Orleans.
