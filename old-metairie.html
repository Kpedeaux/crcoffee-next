The suburbs

Bringing coffee west.

Metairie asked for CR Coffee, and we delivered. Located at 603½ Metairie Road in the heart of Old Metairie, near Metairie Cemetery, this location brings the same Coast Roast Coffee and neighborhood vibe to the west side of the metro. It's a lighter, more efficient space than Magazine Street, but built with the same care.

Whether you're local or passing through, you'll find quality coffee and the kind of welcome that makes coming back easy. Stop by on your way to work or grab a bag of beans to take home.
