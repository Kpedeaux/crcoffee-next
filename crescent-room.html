The space

Your new boardroom.

The room sits inside the Magazine Street shop, in the stretch of Uptown where the river bends around the city. It is a dedicated space you can book for a team meeting, a workshop, a client pitch, or a quiet study day, with the shop and its coffee right outside the door.

A handcrafted conference table, plenty of outlets, and a wall-mounted TV for presentations and video calls. Hot drip coffee, roasted on century-old Royal No. 6 drums, is brewed before you arrive and included with every booking.

Common questions.

Where is The Crescent Room?

Inside CR Coffee Shop at 3618 Magazine St, New Orleans, LA 70115, between Louisiana and Napoleon in Uptown. Free street parking is usually easy to find nearby.

How many people does it seat?

Up to 12 at the conference table.

What does a booking include?

The private room, the conference table, outlets, fast Wi-Fi, the wall-mounted presentation TV, and hot drip coffee. The $185 option adds an assorted pastry tray and in-room coffee service.

How do I book and pay?

Online, at our booking page. Pick an option, choose a date and time, and pay through Square. Sales tax of 10.75% is added at checkout.

Can I reschedule?

Yes, with at least 48 hours notice. Call the shop at (504) 354-9422. Bookings are final and not refunded.
