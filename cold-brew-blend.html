Cold Brew Blend

Rich, smooth, and ready for New Orleans heat.

The blend

The same lush cold brew we pour at the shop.

Brew the same lush cold brew we pour every day at CR Coffee Shop right at home. Our Cold Brew Blend is designed for a full-bodied, chocolate-smooth cup that stays refreshing even when the temperature soars. Fans drink it 12 months out the year because it's that easy-going and satisfying. One 12 oz bag makes a full quart of concentrate. The blend's heavy body and naturally sweet finish let you skip the sugar or dress it up with vanilla cream as you prefer.

Tasting notes.

Smooth cocoa body, hints of dark caramel, clean mellow finish.

Brew method: Cold brew, 18-hour steep

Best for: Cold brew concentrate (cut 1:1 with water or milk)

Brew tip: Grind coarse, steep 18 hours, cut 1:1 with water or milk, and you're good to go.
