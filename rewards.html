How it works.

Spend

Step One

Order at Magazine Street or Old Metairie, in person or online. Every dollar you spend earns you one Bean.

Earn Beans

Step Two

Beans stack up automatically in your Square Loyalty account. No cards to carry, no apps to download. Just your name or phone number at the register.

Redeem

Step Three

Cash in your Beans whenever you're ready. 100 Beans gets you a free drink, up to $7. 200 Beans gets you a free bag of coffee to take home.

Good to know.

Where do I earn Beans?

At Magazine Street and Old Metairie only, in person or online. Rewards are not available at our St. Roch Market stall or the MSY Airport kiosk.

Do I need an app or a card?

No. Give your name or phone number at the register, or type your number on the screen at checkout. You're enrolled instantly and earning Beans on your very first purchase.
